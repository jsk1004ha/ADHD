# Make the choice inspectable

Read the actual decision, goal, options, constraints and existing rationale. For
handoff read [the shared contract](../adhd-native/references/skill-handoff.md).

Eliminate alternatives that violate must-haves before comparing preferences.
Compare the remaining real alternatives by the criteria that matter to this user
and task. Use quantitative comparisons only with sourced measurements or explicitly
justified estimates; do not manufacture scoring weights to force a winner.

Identify which uncertain fact could reverse the recommendation. If an authorized
small check would resolve it, propose or perform that check through the owner.
Otherwise give a conditional recommendation and its conditions. Ask only if the
human's missing priority changes a consequential choice; record and proceed with
an authorized reversible implementation choice.

Return context, selected/recommended option, reasons, rejected alternatives,
negative consequences, unresolved facts and reopen conditions. Mark a proposal
as a proposal. Retain a pointer to superseded reasoning instead of deleting it.
Use the current plan's approach/alternatives/risks for ordinary choices; create a
separate decision artifact only when its reuse justifies it.

Finish when the owner has a supported choice or a concrete missing-evidence task.
Do not reopen a user-settled choice without new conflicting evidence. A decision
record cannot authorize a paid service, unrelated action or extra permission.

Example: “자동 추정과 수동 확인 중 어느 쪽이 나을까?” Compare the actual
traceability constraints, explain uncertainty and what would justify changing the
choice later instead of inventing a weighted score.

## Method inputs

Adapt mandatory/preference criteria and uncertainty from [NASA Decision Analysis](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20080008301/downloads/20080008301.pdf)
and context/consequences/supersession from [Nygard's decision records](https://www.cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions).
