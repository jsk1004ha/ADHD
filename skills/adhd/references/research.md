# Research
Reuse installed `best-practice-research`, `scientific-critical-thinking`, `experimental-design`, `statistical-analysis`, `uncertainty-and-units`, and paper lookup skills as relevant. Use the installed `autoresearch` only when it is the sole outer controller; inside ADHD it may supply methodology, not start another loop.

Separate question, hypothesis, falsifiable test, baseline, experimental factors, fixed conditions, data provenance, analysis and limitations. Retrieve primary sources and record title/author/date/DOI or URL plus the exact supported claim and location in a sources ledger. Check temporal relevance and contradictory evidence. A paper title or search snippet is insufficient support for an unseen result. Do not fabricate citations or fill missing empirical data with plausible values.

Before running costly experiments, freeze train/validation/test splits and budget; protect test data from tuning. Check data quality, leakage, seeds, units, uncertainty and baseline fairness. Save code/config/environment plus raw outputs before making claims. Numerical, logical and bibliographic validation must be distinguishable. Report negative results and uncertainty; never optimize toward the user's hoped-for conclusion by changing evidence.

Deliver a reproducible research note, sources ledger and necessary code/config; supply a report only when requested. Bound experiments by runs, wall time and compute budget. A failed research hypothesis can be a successfully completed honest investigation, but cannot count as a positive experimental finding.

A previous recipe can reuse acquisition and validation steps; it cannot substitute for fresh literature or fresh experimental measurements.
